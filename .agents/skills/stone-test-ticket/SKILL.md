# Stone Test Ticket

Use execution/prompts/test_engineer.md or test_review.md for the assigned role. The coordinator runs approved selectors through tools/execution/gates.py. Inspect actual JUnit, fixture isolation, before/during/after health and the scenario contributions. Missing, skipped, mock-only or stale evidence cannot pass. Report missing tests for developer repair; do not modify a reviewed candidate or mark the whole feature accepted from a partial ticket.
